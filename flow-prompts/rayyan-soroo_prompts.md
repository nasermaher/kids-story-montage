# حزمة برومبتات الصور — «ريّان وسورو» (Google Flow / Nano Banana)

> البرومبتات بالإنجليزية (النماذج تفهمها أدق)، والشرح بالعربية. **لا نصوص داخل الصور**: الحروف العربية تتشوّه، والنص يُركّب لاحقاً على الفيديو أو الكتاب.
> **افتراضات** يمكن تغييرها: نمط «كتاب أطفال مائي ناعم»، نسبة 16:9، 11 مشهداً مطابقة لـ `story/story_ar.md`.

## 0) الكتل الثابتة (تُلصق حرفياً في كل برومبت)

**STYLE**
```
Soft watercolor children's picture-book illustration, gentle rounded shapes, warm paper texture, clean simple backgrounds, big friendly eyes, no outlines harsher than a soft brown line, calm and cozy mood.
```
**PALETTE:** `warm cream, wood brown, sunshine yellow, soft sky blue (all others only as light tints)`
**RAYYAN**
```
Rayyan, a 4-year-old boy, light tan skin, short curly dark-brown hair, round cheeks with a faint blush, wearing a sunshine-yellow t-shirt and blue shorts, barefoot, small and sweet.
```
**SOROO**
```
Soroo, a handmade wooden Japanese soroban (abacus) character: warm honey-brown wooden frame, five vertical rods, one upper bead and four lower beads on each rod, creamy-white round beads, a small smiling face with two big kind eyes and rosy cheeks painted on the top bar of the frame. No arms or legs.
```
**NO-TEXT:** `No text, no letters, no numerals, no watermark anywhere in the image.`
**قاعدة الخرزات** (أضِفها لأي مشهد يُظهر رقماً): *only the rightmost rod is in use; on that rod the beads touching the center beam are shown in warm golden color to count the number; all other rods are fully at rest, far from the beam.*

## 1) خطوات التهيئة في Flow
1. ولّد **صفحة مرجع الأسلوب** (A) ثم **ورقة شخصية ريّان** (B) ثم **ورقة شخصية سورو** (C). اختر أفضل نتيجة لكل واحدة.
2. في كل مشهد لاحق **أرفق B وC** كصور مرجعية وألصق كتل RAYYAN وSOROO نصاً أيضاً.
3. اختر النسبة **16:9** عند كل توليد (النص وحده لا يحدّدها).
4. راجع: لون القميص والشعر ثابت؟ عدد الخرزات صحيح؟ إن انحرف تفصيل، عدّل جملته فقط في البرومبت. الواجهة تتغير، فتحقق من الخيارات الحالية في Flow.

### A — مرجع الأسلوب
```
STYLE. A tiny still life on a wooden table in a warm cozy room: a wooden soroban abacus and a sunshine-yellow cup beside a sunlit window. Palette: warm cream, wood brown, sunshine yellow, soft sky blue. NO-TEXT.
```
### B — ورقة شخصية ريّان
```
STYLE. Character sheet of RAYYAN on a plain cream background: full body front view, side view, and three face expressions (curious with a small round open mouth, happy big smile, sad with a gentle frown). Same outfit in all views. Evenly lit, no scenery. NO-TEXT.
```
### C — ورقة شخصية سورو
```
STYLE. Character sheet of SOROO on a plain cream background: front view, three-quarter view, and face close-ups: smiling, closed happy eyes (sleepy), and a gentle head tilt. Beads all at rest. NO-TEXT.
```

## 2) برومبتات المشاهد (16:9 · المراجع: B + C)

> كل برومبت = `STYLE` + `RAYYAN` + `SOROO` + الوصف + `NO-TEXT`. أسفل الصورة (~20%) يبقى هادئاً وفارغاً لموضع النص لاحقاً.

**01 — اللقاء**
```
[STYLE] [RAYYAN] [SOROO] Rayyan stands on tiptoe beside a wooden table, looking with wide curious eyes at Soroo, who stands upright on the table smiling at him. Warm room, sunlit window, soft light. Beads all at rest. Keep the bottom 20% calm and empty. [NO-TEXT]
```
**02 — الصفر (٠)**
```
[STYLE] [RAYYAN] [SOROO] Rayyan laughs and waves both empty hands in the air beside Soroo. Every bead on every rod is far from the center beam (zero). Bottom 20% calm and empty. [NO-TEXT]
```
**03 — واحد (١)**
```
[STYLE] [RAYYAN] [SOROO] Rayyan gently pushes ONE lower bead up to the center beam with his small index finger. Exactly one golden bead touches the beam on the rightmost rod, others at rest. Soroo smiles encouragingly. Bottom 20% calm. [NO-TEXT]
```
**04 — اثنان (٢)**
```
[STYLE] [RAYYAN] [SOROO] Rayyan pushes the second lower bead up: exactly TWO golden beads touch the beam on the rightmost rod. Soroo's eyes sparkle with tiny stars. Bottom 20% calm. [NO-TEXT]
```
**05 — ثلاثة (٣)**
```
[STYLE] [RAYYAN] [SOROO] Exactly THREE golden lower beads touch the beam on the rightmost rod. Rayyan jumps joyfully in the air with arms raised, hair bouncing. Soroo beams. Bottom 20% calm. [NO-TEXT]
```
**06 — أربعة (٤)**
```
[STYLE] [RAYYAN] [SOROO] All FOUR lower beads on the rightmost rod touch the beam, golden; the upper bead stays away. Rayyan holds four fingers up proudly. Soroo looks pleased. Bottom 20% calm. [NO-TEXT]
```
**07 — خمسة (٥)**
```
[STYLE] [RAYYAN] [SOROO] Rayyan slides the single UPPER bead down onto the center beam (golden) on the rightmost rod; all four lower beads rest far from the beam. He looks amazed at the new bead. Bottom 20% calm. [NO-TEXT]
```
**08 — الخطأ (ستة؟)**
```
[STYLE] [RAYYAN] [SOROO] On the rightmost rod the upper bead AND all four lower beads touch the beam (too many, the child's mistake). Rayyan sighs with a small frown, shoulders slightly down. Soroo tilts her head kindly, no harshness. Bottom 20% calm. [NO-TEXT]
```
**09 — ستة (٦) صحيحة**
```
[STYLE] [RAYYAN] [SOROO] Correct six: the upper bead and exactly ONE lower bead touch the beam on the rightmost rod (golden), three lower beads far away. Rayyan smiles proudly, eyes bright; Soroo glows with warmth. Bottom 20% calm. [NO-TEXT]
```
**10 — سبعة وثمانية وتسعة (٧ ٨ ٩)**
```
[STYLE] [RAYYAN] [SOROO] Three small vignettes in one wide picture, left to right on the same table: upper bead + 2 lower beads, upper bead + 3 lower beads, upper bead + 4 lower beads, always on the rightmost rod, golden. Rayyan grows more confident in each vignette. Bottom 20% calm. [NO-TEXT]
```
**11 — الختام**
```
[STYLE] [RAYYAN] [SOROO] Evening, dusk light through the window (soft orange and violet). Rayyan sleeps peacefully with a small smile, head resting on his arms on the table beside Soroo; Soroo has sleepy closed eyes and a gentle smile. A few golden beads at rest. Bottom 20% calm. [NO-TEXT]
```

## 3) الغلاف (اختياري)
```
[STYLE] [RAYYAN] [SOROO] Cover scene: Rayyan and Soroo side by side, Rayyan hugging Soroo's frame with one hand, both smiling at the viewer, warm window light. Leave the top 30% calm and empty for the title. [NO-TEXT]
```

## 4) بعد التوليد
